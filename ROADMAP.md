# Veil Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation

Finalize product boundaries, privacy guarantees, architecture decisions, domain state machine, permission education, and test strategy. Establish the Android 12+ baseline and the supported-device matrix.

## Phase 1 — MVP

Build onboarding, permission coordination, protection levels, local settings, foreground monitoring service, in-memory capture pipeline, TensorFlow Lite inference adapter, intervention overlay, cooldowns, recovery tasks, lock periods, local history, and failure-state notifications.

## Phase 2 — Beta hardening

Measure latency, battery, memory, crashes, false positives, false negatives, reboot recovery, permission revocation, and OEM behavior. Run a closed beta with explicit privacy communication and structured feedback.

## Phase 3 — Trust and distribution

Complete privacy policy, Accessibility declaration, Data Safety form, permission justification, security review, transparency documentation, and Google Play readiness. Maintain direct APK distribution as a fallback.

## Phase 4 — Expansion

Only after the core experience is reliable, consider adjacent commitments such as focus, sleep, doomscrolling, and goal enforcement. New categories must preserve local processing, explicit consent, and the same intervention ethics.

## Deferred

Remote accountability, cloud image processing, screenshot history, full uninstall prevention, and features that require covert or third-party monitoring are explicitly deferred or prohibited.
