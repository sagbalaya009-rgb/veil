# Founder Memo

Veil exists to help people keep commitments they have made to themselves. Its advantage is not surveillance, data extraction, or coercion. Its advantage is a trustworthy intervention that is visible, local, and intentionally difficult to bypass in an impulsive moment.

The founding rule is simple: **Veil protects the user from the moment, not the user from themselves.** The product should create a pause, offer a recovery action, and return control to the person. Every technical and business decision should reinforce that relationship.

Trust is the product. Privacy is not a feature tier. The team must prefer a smaller product with stronger guarantees over a larger product that requires invasive access or ambiguous data practices.
